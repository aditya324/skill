About SkillTern Education

Bridging the gap between education and employment

SkillTern Education is a pioneering EdTech platform dedicated to bridging the gap between education and employment by offering hands-on, industry-immersive learning experiences.

With a mission to equip students with real-world skills and career opportunities, SkillTern Education collaborates with corporate partners, industry experts, and practitioners to curate cutting-edge courses embedded with internships and live project use cases.

Our Vision

To redefine education by making industry-integrated, career-focused learning the new standard—ensuring every learner is ready for today's opportunities and tomorrow's challenges.

Our Mission

To deliver practitioner-led, internship-embedded, and industry-certified learning experiences that equip learners with practical skills, professional exposure, and the confidence to thrive in the careers of tomorrow.
